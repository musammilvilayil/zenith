How to choose

Start with one real problem and one measurable outcome.

A strong final-year project is not the one with the most modules. It is the one that works reliably, has realistic inputs, produces evidence and can be clearly explained during review and viva.

01

Select

Choose a problem and users your team can actually access.

02

Scope

Limit the MVP to one workflow and one demonstrable result.

03

Build

Create working logic, data flow and interfaces—not screens alone.

04

Validate

Test with realistic records, users, sensors or scenarios.

05

Present

Document architecture, results, limitations and future scope.
