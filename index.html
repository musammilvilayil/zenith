Ask us directly ↗
What can I discuss with Zenith?

You can enquire about business websites, e-commerce and booking experiences, web or mobile applications, interface work, and ongoing website support. We will confirm fit and scope after understanding your brief.

How does a project usually begin?

Start with the contact form and describe your goal, timeline and any existing materials. We can then clarify requirements and discuss a suitable next step.

Do I need a complete specification?

No. A clear description of the problem or idea is a useful starting point. We can identify the open questions together before defining the scope.

Can you work with an existing website or app?

Yes, you can ask about improving an existing site or application. Share the current setup, what is not working and the outcome you want; feasibility depends on the project.

How can I get a project estimate?

Send the details you know through the project enquiry form. An estimate depends on scope, functionality, integrations and delivery requirements, so we discuss those before confirming a figure.
