A structured process

From consultation to confident handover

01 · Days 1–3

Consult

Bring your idea or request help choosing one. Discuss feasibility, goals and college deadlines.

02 · Days 4–7

Plan

Agree modules, responsibilities, scope, schedule, training and milestone payments.

03 · Days 8–30

Develop & learn

Build in stages, review demos, understand modules and provide feedback.

04 · Days 31–40

Test & practice

Verify functionality, fix agreed issues, practise walkthroughs and review presentations.

05 · Days 41–45

Handover

Receive agreed deliverables and guidance, with a defined post-delivery support period.

Why start early? More time to explore the application, understand each module, test scenarios and prepare for reviews without last-minute pressure. The 30–45 day schedule is an estimate, subject to complexity, feedback and availability.
